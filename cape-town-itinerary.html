Kirstenbosch National Botanical Garden
Five minutes from Newlands Peak — never done before, so the natural pick. Tree Canopy (Boomslang) Walkway, the sculpture trail, and the on-site shop for locally made design and decor. Lunch at the Kirstenbosch Tea Room or Vinyl Café, garden-view.
🕒 08:00–18:00 (winter)💵 R110pp SA resident · R270 int'l
Zeitz MOCAA · Silo District
Contemporary African art in a striking converted grain silo at the Waterfront — architecture and art in one stop. A free guided tour runs at 12:30pm on Fridays — book on arrival at the Front of House desk, capacity is limited. Parking at the Silo District Garage (P3) has direct museum access. Worth noting: you're back at the Waterfront tomorrow for lunch and the Watershed, so this doubles up on the area, just a different building and a different kind of browsing.
🕒 10:00–18:00 · last entry 17:30💵 R265pp · under-18s free
Kloof Street design crawl & brunch
Ten minutes from Newlands into the City Bowl. Independent design and decor stores, concept shops and galleries the length of Kloof St, with a proper sit-down brunch built in — The Power & the Glory, Clarke's, or Skinny Legs & All are all on the strip.
🕒 Shops ~09:00–17:00 · free to browse💵 Brunch ~R150–250pp

A flat, easy coastal walk along the promenade with Lion's Head as a backdrop, ending at Mojo Market — an indoor food and design market with local stalls, coffee roasters and craft goods. Relaxed and very walkable together.
