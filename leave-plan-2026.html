Notes on this plan: Sep is shown as 4 days rather than 3 — the Heritage Day bridge is too good to waste with just 1 day (Fri 25). Taking Mon–Wed gives you a full 9-day stretch for the price of 4 leave days. The 6-day buffer gives you room for unexpected sick days, family commitments, or a spontaneous long weekend without blowing the budget. The December trip costs only 6 working days because the trip departs on a Sunday, Christmas and Day of Goodwill eat two working days, and the return (15 Jan) falls in the 2027 leave year. If your HR system requires pre-approval for blocks over 5 days, flag August and September early.
