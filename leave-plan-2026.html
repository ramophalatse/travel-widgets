Notes on this plan: Annual budget is 45 days. Already-taken figures for June (8 days), July (3 days) and August (2 days) match the Leave Summary dashboard, which is the source of truth for actual dates. September is 3 days: the 10–11 Sep long weekend plus Fri 25 Sep, which turns Heritage Day into a 4-day weekend (the earlier Mon–Wed bridge was dropped). November adds Thu 5 and Fri 6 Nov around the local government elections on Wed 4 Nov, an effective public holiday, for a 5-day break before December. December is 9 days: Fri 18 Dec for the weekend away, then 8 days of the family trip. The family trip needs 18 leave days in total: we're away from Mon 21 Dec 2026 until landing on Fri 15 Jan 2027. 8 of those days fall in December 2026 (Christmas is the only public holiday that saves a day, since Day of Goodwill is a Saturday), and 10 fall in January 2027 (New Year's Day saves one). The January days don't touch the 2026 buffer, but they use 10 of the 2027 budget in the first fortnight of the year, so apply for the whole block as one request. Swapping three September days for November and 18 Dec leaves the 2026 total unchanged: 42 of 45 days, with 3 days of buffer.
